Crypto After October Crisis
A professional research blog focused on analyzing cryptocurrency markets after a major October crisis. Combines long-form analytical writing with a research paper discovery system that indexes crypto research reports and helps detect emerging trends.
Live Demo
View the live application:
https://firaseth.github.io/crypto-after-october-crisis/
📖 Overview
This project is a comprehensive research platform designed for crypto traders, investors, and market analysts. It features a semantic search engine for research papers, AI-powered summarization (simulated), and a trend analysis dashboard.

Target Audience
Crypto Traders looking for data-driven market signals.
Crypto Investors seeking long-term structural analysis.
Market Analysts requiring aggregated research sources.
Research Focus
All research revolves around structural changes in cryptocurrency markets following a major crisis:

Market crash analysis
Liquidity collapse and recovery
Institutional behavior changes
DeFi market stability
Exchange solvency
Capital flow shifts
✨ Features
Semantic Search Interface: Search research papers by meaning and context.
Research Indexing: Indexed reports from Messari, Glassnode, Chainalysis, Dune Analytics, and Nansen.
AI Summarization (Simulated): Automated extraction of key thesis, data sources, and conclusions.
Trend Detection Dashboard: Visualizing emerging themes in crypto research.
Topic Clustering: Automatic grouping of papers into sectors (DeFi, Stablecoins, Institutional, etc.).
Responsive Design: Professional financial publication aesthetic with a dark theme.
